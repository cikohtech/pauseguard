# PauseGuard explanation clarity rating

20 explanations PauseGuard showed when it paused (AMBER) or held (RED) a payment in benchmark run `20261015T0334Z_local_test`, picked at random. Everything is synthetic.

**For each one, imagine you were about to send this payment and the bank showed you this message.** Rate 1–5: *How clearly does it tell you why the payment was stopped and what to do next?* (1 = I don't understand it, 5 = completely clear). A comment is optional. Please ask 3–5 people, including at least one aged 60 or over.

Save the answers as `eval/human_ratings.csv` with the columns `rater,rater_age_band,item,clarity,comment` (age band: under 40, 40-59, 60+), one row per rater and item, then run `make eval-report`.
